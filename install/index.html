BEFORE YOU CONNECT

A few simple steps.

  1. 1

    Choose the exact model

    Use the display name printed on your board. The installer checks the connected chip family. For GUITION, confirm a P4 revision below 3.0 and compatible C6 radio firmware before installing.

  2. 2

    Connect over USB

    Use a data cable and close any serial monitor. Select the display’s USB port when your browser asks.

  3. 3

    Install, then restart

    Keep the display powered until installation finishes. Use its touchscreen to connect Wi-Fi and your bike afterward.

Already running Wheelie Fun Display?

Use Settings → Firmware updates for normal updates. A complete USB installation replaces the flash layout and can reset saved settings.

Port missing? Check your USB data cable and follow your board’s download-mode instructions. For Waveshare, hold BOOT, tap RESET, then release BOOT. SeeUSB installation instructionsfor board setup and first connection.
